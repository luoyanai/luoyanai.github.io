About

Researcher Profile

My research sits at the intersection of mechanical engineering, robot control, and AI-driven decision making. I develop systems that translate high-level visual and semantic knowledge into executable physical behaviors for humanoids, mobile manipulators, and collaborative robotic platforms.

My recent work centers on making foundation-model feedback useful for robot learning and perception. In MotionVL, visual-language evaluation and LLM-generated rewards are integrated into humanoid reinforcement learning to improve motion quality and real-robot robustness. In TOVEP, RGB-D observations are transformed into enhanced depth, structural support, and confidence cues for picking-point localization in cluttered fruit scenes.

TOVEP: Visual-Geometric Evidence Enhancement for VLM-Guided Fruit Picking-Point Localization

Yan Luo et al.

TOVEP enhances RGB-D evidence for VLM-guided picking-point localization by producing an enhanced depth map, structural support map, and confidence map. A compact top-k structural reference module guides the VLM toward likely fruit-stem attachment regions and returns a structured picking record with point, approach orientation, confidence, and supporting evidence.

MotionVL: Visual-Language Supervision for Guiding Reinforcement Learning in Humanoid Motion

Yan Luo et al.

A framework that uses vision-language models and large language models to provide semantic supervision and generate reward functions for humanoid reinforcement learning, improving success rate, robustness, energy efficiency, and motion naturalness.

DCM-based Dynamic Stable Walking under Terrain-Induced Time-Varying Disturbances for Humanoid Robots

Yan Luo et al.

A DCM-based time-varying disturbance walking method that adjusts center-of-mass height and stride height through variable height stabilization and N-step capturability, enabling stable humanoid walking under abrupt terrain inclination changes.

Language-Guided Humanoid Motion Control
