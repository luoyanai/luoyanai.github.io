Yan Luo

In September 2026, I earned my Ph.D. at the Institute of Robotics, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My advisors were Han Ding (Member of the Chinese Academy of Sciences) and Zhenhua Xiong (Professor at Shanghai Jiao Tong University).

I work on self-improving robots that continually learn from physical experience.

I combine model-based control, reinforcement learning, and vision-language feedback for humanoid locomotion and manipulation. My real-to-sim-to-real approach connects interactive digital twins, policy learning, and hardware deployment, with physical experiments informing both modeling and policy design.

My focus is recursive self-improvement (RSI), using generative world models to turn real-world successes and failures into targeted practice, enabling robots to identify skill gaps and decide what to learn next.

MotionVL: Vision-Language Supervision for Reinforcement Learning of Humanoid Motion

Yan Luo, Jianhua Wu, Zhenhua Xiong, and Han Ding

IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters · 11(5), 5717–5724 · 2026 · IROS 2026 oral presentation

MotionVL uses descriptions of simulated motion to generate reward functions. We evaluated jumping, running, and single-leg balance in simulation and demonstrated straight-leg walking on hardware.
