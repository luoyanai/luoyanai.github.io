Postdoctoral Research Portfolio

Yan Luo

Ph.D. Candidate · Humanoid Robotics and Embodied AI

Expected Ph.D. completion: October 2026

I work on humanoid robotics and embodied intelligence, connecting vision-language models, reinforcement learning, and whole-body control to build robots that can perceive, reason, act, and improve through real-world interaction.

Postdoctoral direction. My postdoctoral interests include foundation models for robotics, in-context learning (ICL), and recursive self-improvement (RSI). Building on MotionVL, I aim to study how embodied agents use context and feedback to adapt and improve.

Research agenda

Perceive, reason, and act in the physical world

My research connects multimodal perception, robot learning, and whole-body control.

MotionVL uses vision-language evaluation and generated rewards for humanoid motion learning, while TOVEP structures RGB-D evidence for VLM-guided picking-point localization. My work on dynamic walking complements these approaches with methods for maintaining balance under changing terrain conditions.

TOVEP: Visual-Geometric Evidence Enhancement for VLM-Guided Fruit Picking-Point Localization

Yan Luo, Zexi Bo, Ruonan Liu, Zhenhua Xiong, and Han Ding

Computers and Electronics in Agriculture · 2026 · Manuscript under review

Structures RGB-D observations into depth, structural-support, and confidence cues for VLM-guided picking-point localization. Evaluated across multiple crops against RGB-only and raw-depth baselines without VLM fine-tuning.

Video

MotionVL: Vision-Language Supervision for Reinforcement Learning of Humanoid Motion

Yan Luo, Jianhua Wu, Zhenhua Xiong, and Han Ding

IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters · 11(5), 5717–5724 · 2026

Combines vision-language motion evaluation and LLM-generated rewards for humanoid reinforcement learning, with validation in simulation and real-robot locomotion.

DCM-Based Dynamic Stable Walking under Terrain-Induced Time-Varying Disturbances for Humanoid Robots

Yan Luo, Chao Liu, Jianhua Wu, Zhenhua Xiong, and Han Ding

Science China Technological Sciences · 68(4), 1420301 · 2025

Combines variable-height stabilization with DCM control and N-step capturability for humanoid walking under time-varying terrain disturbances, validated in simulation and physical experiments.

Image Foreground Segmentation Based on Small Data Set for Visual Servo Applications

Yan Luo, Gaoming Chen, Chao Liu, and Zhenhua Xiong

IEEE/ASME International Conference on Advanced Intelligent Mechatronics · 715–720 · 2023

A small-data foreground segmentation method for visual servo applications, combining detection, semantic segmentation, feature comparison, and neural-network control.

Language-Guided Beverage Serving
