Postdoctoral Research Portfolio

Yan Luo

Ph.D. Candidate · Humanoid Robotics and Embodied AI

I work on humanoid robotics and embodied intelligence, connecting vision-language models, reinforcement learning, and whole-body control to build robots capable of perception, reasoning, in-context learning (ICL), and recursive self-improvement (RSI) through real-world interaction.

Postdoctoral direction. I am interested in foundation models for robotics, in-context learning (ICL), and recursive self-improvement (RSI) for embodied agents. Building on MotionVL and my work in multimodal perception and control, I aim to develop robots that learn from context, evaluate their own behavior, and improve through interaction.

Research agenda

Perceive, reason, and act in the physical world

My research sits at the intersection of mechanical engineering, robot control, and AI-driven decision making. I develop systems that translate high-level visual and semantic knowledge into executable physical behaviors for humanoids, mobile manipulators, and collaborative robotic platforms.

My recent work centers on making foundation-model feedback useful for robot learning and perception. In MotionVL, visual-language evaluation and LLM-generated rewards are integrated into humanoid reinforcement learning to improve motion quality and real-robot robustness. In TOVEP, RGB-D observations are transformed into enhanced depth, structural support, and confidence cues for picking-point localization in cluttered fruit scenes.

My target postdoctoral work connects foundation models, in-context robot learning, and reliable deployment. MotionVL provides a starting point for studying how multimodal evaluation and generated feedback can support adaptation and recursive self-improvement in physical systems.

TOVEP: Visual-Geometric Evidence Enhancement for VLM-Guided Fruit Picking-Point Localization

Yan Luo, Zexi Bo, Ruonan Liu, Zhenhua Xiong, and Han Ding

A visual–geometric evidence enhancement framework that boosts multimodal VLM perception for fruit harvesting: RGB-D is converted into enhanced depth, structural support, and confidence cues, then focused into attachment-region references so the model can more reliably localize picking points and approach orientations.

Demo Video
Summary. TOVEP gives a general-purpose VLM structured geometric evidence before asking it to localize a physical action. Its value lies in converting noisy RGB-D observations into interpretable support and confidence cues that remain connected to an executable picking pose.
RGB-D evidence enhancement Vision-language model Picking-point localization

Yan Luo, Jianhua Wu, Zhenhua Xiong, and Han Ding

A vision-language and LLM-guided reinforcement learning framework for humanoid motion, using semantic supervision and generated rewards to improve robustness and naturalness.

DCM-based Dynamic Stable Walking under Terrain-Induced Time-Varying Disturbances for Humanoid Robots

Yan Luo, Chao Liu, Jianhua Wu, Zhenhua Xiong, and Han Ding

A DCM-based walking method with variable-height stabilization and N-step capturability for stable humanoid locomotion under abrupt terrain inclination changes.

Robotics Portfolio

Representative Systems and Demonstrations

Outdoor Humanoid Fruit Picking

Outdoor validation of humanoid mobility and manipulation in an unstructured tree-canopy scene, where visibility, reachability, and body balance must be handled together. The demo connects fruit picking-point localization with full-body action, showing a path from multimodal perception to deployable field robotics.

Real-World Retail Picking

Real-robot manipulation in a constrained retail setting with shelves, glass doors, counters, and object clutter. The system integrates target grounding, reach-aware planning, collision-conscious motion, and precise execution to complete multi-stage picking tasks reliably in dense, human-centered environments.

Language-Guided Beverage Serving

Language-conditioned tabletop manipulation requiring object grounding, grasp sequencing, pouring geometry, and fine end-effector control. The task illustrates how high-level instructions can be converted into physically grounded action chains where perception, planning, and contact execution all matter.

Whole-Body Humanoid Teleoperation

VR teleoperation system for mapping human upper-body and hand motions to a humanoid platform under realistic actuation limits. It provides a practical data engine for imitation learning, policy initialization, and evaluating how human intent transfers to whole-body robot control.

Disturbance-Resilient Humanoid Walking

Dynamic balance validation on a shaking and tilting support surface, targeting terrain-induced disturbances that break nominal walking controllers. The demo emphasizes controllable recovery through foot placement, center-of-mass regulation, and body-state stabilization under time-varying contacts.

Retail Manipulation in Simulation

Convenience-store simulation used to test perception, target selection, grasp planning, and placement policies across repeatable interaction cases. The setting is useful for scaling evaluation before real-robot transfer, especially for tasks that require semantic understanding and precise spatial manipulation.

Digital Twin Smart-Factory with Embodied Robots

A digital-twin smart-factory scenario that digitizes the plant floor and couples it with robot simulation, treating embodied robots as deployable agents in industrial workflows. The demonstration connects scene understanding, task allocation, and multi-robot coordination in a simulated production environment, bridging isolated robot skills toward integrated manufacturing deployment.

Humanoid Piano Performance and Media Coverage

Publicly reported humanoid piano demonstration focusing on dexterous hand motion, upper-body coordination, and repeatable fine manipulation. Its value is as a visible stress test for actuation precision, timing, and expressive robot behavior in a task that non-specialist audiences can immediately assess.
