Humanoid walking on moving terrain

When a platform tilts as a foot lands, regaining balance can take several steps. Our DCM-TVDW controller adjusts where and when a humanoid steps, as well as how high it carries its body and swing foot.

Yan Luo, Chao Liu, Jianhua Wu, Zhenhua Xiong, and Han Ding

PaperVideo

Recovering over several steps

The divergent component of motion (DCM) captures how the body moves away from a stable stance. We combine it with N-step capturability to plan a sequence of footholds that can bring the robot back into balance. A variable-height controller adapts the center of mass and foot clearance to changes in terrain.

Walking on a shaking platform

We tested the controller in simulation and on the SJ-Bruce robot. In the physical experiment, the robot crossed a moving platform whose inclination reached approximately 22°.
