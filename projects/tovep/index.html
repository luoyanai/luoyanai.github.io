TOVEP

Where should a robot approach a fruit when leaves hide the stem? TOVEP uses color and depth to help a vision-language model find the fruit’s attachment point and propose a direction of approach.

Yan Luo, Zexi Bo, Ruonan Liu, Zhenhua Xiong, and Han Ding

Paper Video 3D scene Dataset

Finding a fruit’s attachment point

TOVEP refines the depth map, identifies structures around potential attachment points, and estimates which regions have reliable evidence. A vision-language model examines these candidate regions to select a picking point and approach direction. Depth and camera calibration place the point in the camera’s coordinate frame.

Testing across four crops

The public benchmark contains 220 expert-annotated RGB-D frames, with 55 each for mango, orange, pepper, and strawberry. We reserved these frames for final evaluation. Mean picking-point error was 29.2 pixels with TOVEP and 121.2 pixels when the vision-language model received only the color image.
